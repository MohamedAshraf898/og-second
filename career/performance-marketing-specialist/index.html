Performance Marketing Specialist (remote & full-time)

At VIKST, we’re looking for a Performance Marketing Specialist who understands what it means to act as a growth partner - someone who can think strategically, communicate clearly, and proactively guide eCommerce clients toward real, measurable growth.

You’ll manage and optimise paid social campaigns (primarily Meta), but also go beyond platform execution - analysing performance, understanding business goals and advising on growth levers.

This is a full-time remote role.

What you’ll be working on:

  • Plan, execute, and scale paid campaigns on Meta (Instagram & Facebook)
  • Collaborate with content and dev teams to align ads, creatives, and landing pages
  • Analyse performance, identify bottlenecks, and adjust strategies to improve results
  • Communicate with clients through text (no client calls)
  • Act as a true growth partner - not just an ad operator
  • Provide clear reporting and data-driven insights
  • Optimise ads across secondary channels, including Snapchat, TikTok, and Google.

We’re looking for someone who:

  • Has solid experience with Meta Ads
  • Has excellent written and spoken communication skills in English
  • Is proactive, analytical, and structured in their thinking
  • Can confidently advise clients, not just execute
  • Experience with Snapchat Ads, TikTok Ads, or Google Ads (not required but big plus)

What you’ll get:

  • A fully remote role
  • Direct responsibility for exciting eCommerce brands in the UAE and GCC
  • Access to our internal VIKST Academy with high-level marketing training
  • A motivated, young, and collaborative team
  • Paid team workcations once a year
  • Competitive salary based on experience and impact

Additional details:

  • Full-time and remote
  • Start: As soon as possible
